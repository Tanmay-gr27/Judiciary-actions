Guardian of the Constitution

The Constitution of India establishes three co-equal branches of government. While the legislature makes laws and the executive implements them, the judiciary stands as the ultimate interpreter. It is the "watchdog of democracy," ensuring every government action aligns with the supreme law of the land. The independence of the judiciary is the very bedrock of the rule of law in India.

Pillars of Judicial Power

The judiciary exercises its authority through two profound powers. These actions allow it to safeguard the constitution and actively deliver justice when other branches of government fall short.

Judicial Review

This is the power of the Supreme Court and High Courts to examine any law or government action. If an action is found to violate the principles of the Constitution, it can be declared unconstitutional and void. This power serves as the ultimate check on the legislative and executive branches, ensuring they operate within their constitutional limits.

Judicial Activism

This represents the judiciary's proactive role in protecting citizens' rights and promoting social justice, especially when the legislature or executive fails to act. The most powerful tool for this is Public Interest Litigation (PIL), which allows any citizen to bring a matter of public concern before the court, making justice accessible to even the most marginalized communities.

Landmark Cases in Action

These pivotal cases demonstrate how judicial review and activism have shaped Indian democracy. Select a case to explore its background, the court's decision, and its lasting impact.

Kesavananda Bharati v. Kerala

Established the 'Basic Structure' Doctrine, limiting Parliament's power to amend the Constitution.

Hussainara Khatoon v. Bihar

Affirmed the right to a speedy trial as a fundamental right through a PIL.

Vishaka v. Rajasthan

Created legally binding guidelines against workplace sexual harassment in the absence of legislation.

Power, Responsibility, and Trust

With immense power comes great responsibility. Judges adhere to the highest ethical standards to maintain public trust, the judiciary's greatest asset. Through judicial review, it acts as the Constitution's guardian, and through judicial activism, it has become a powerful voice for the voiceless. By balancing its powers with a commitment to ethics, the Indian judiciary continues to play an indispensable role in strengthening our democracy.
